WHY THIS PAPER
The study directly examines skill extraction from resumes, where layout,
wording, and the definition of a skill can vary. It compares automated
approaches with human annotations collected across 46 resumes. Its focus
on evidence and interpretation is relevant to a transparent screening tool.

HOW IT RELATES TO THIS PROJECT
The app uses its own deterministic skill catalog and alias rules. Reviewers
can edit the target skill list and inspect the matching text. These design
choices address the practical need to review automated matches in context.
The paper provides research context and does not validate the app's scores,
ranking, or estimated ATS readiness. Its dataset and methods were not used
to train or benchmark this implementation.
